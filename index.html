Anatomy of Head and Neck

Your three-step revision path

For each topic, the same three tools take you from recognising the right answer to writing it yourself. Work through them in order: each step prepares the next one.

The three levels

1

Multiple-choice warm-up

Single-answer questions with a full explanation after each one.

Use it to recognise the facts. Start in Practice mode, then try Exam mode (timed, no feedback until the end). Flashcards are handy on the go. “My mistakes” replays what you missed.

2

Fill in the blanks

Short texts with key words removed, in two levels.

Level 1 gives a word bank: pick the missing words. Level 2 has no list: type them. Small spelling slips are accepted, and you can overrule the check if you were right.

3

Open questions

The exam format: you write the answer yourself.

Use it to write like the exam. Answer in your own words, read the model answer, then tick the key points you covered. Anything below 70% goes to “To review”.

Suggested rhythm for two weeks: multiple-choice on days 1 to 3, fill in the blanks on days 4 to 8 (word bank first, then typed), open questions on days 9 to 14. Finish each day by replaying “My mistakes” or “To review”.

Good to know

  • Your progress is saved in your browser on this device. Using another device or browser starts fresh, and clearing site data erases it.
  • Everything is built from the course slides only. Where a slide looks inconsistent, the explanation says “according to the slides”: check with your professor which version they expect.
  • Open questions are self-assessed: be strict when you tick the key points.

Not a substitute for the lectures: use it to check what you know and fix what you don’t.
