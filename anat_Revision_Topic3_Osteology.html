FossaBoundaries and contents

Anterior: frontal process of the zygomatic and zygomatic process of the frontal. Points to watch in the slides

  • Spelling varies in the slides (e.g. "Esphenoid", "Pterigopalatine", "orbitary"). Use the standard English: sphenoid, pterygopalatine, orbital.
  • The maxilla slide says "Lateral wall: zygomatic process of the maxillary process"; read it as the zygomatic process of the maxilla.
  • The temporal fossa slide mentions the "frontal process of the zygomatic bone" and the "zygomatic process of the frontal bone": keep both wordings straight.
  • Foramen rotundum, ovale and spinosum contents are not on the slides; confirm with your professor if they are expected.
