Important anatomical corrections to the lecture notes
  • Genioglossus: chiefly protrudes and depresses the tongue; it does not retract it.
  • Pharyngeal constrictors: motor supply is mainly CN X via the pharyngeal plexus. Stylopharyngeus is supplied by CN IX.
  • Digastric: anterior belly is V3; posterior belly is CN VII.
  • Tongue: CN XII supplies all intrinsic and extrinsic muscles except palatoglossus (CN X). Intrinsic muscle actions are distinguished in the model answers.
  • Parotid gland: secretomotor supply comes from CN IX via the otic ganglion and auriculotemporal nerve (V3), not from the facial nerve passing through the gland.
  • Maxillary sinus: drains into the middle meatus; the natural ostium is relatively high on its medial wall.
